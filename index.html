Our Story

At Velora, we believe that a candle is more than just wax and a wick; it's a sensory experience, a moment of pause, and a touch of elegance for your sanctuary. Born in India out of a passion for artisan craftsmanship, our candles are hand-poured in small batches using premium natural soy wax, lead-free cotton wicks, and carefully blended fragrance oils. Let our glowing warmth and captivating aromas turn your everyday moments into exceptional memories.
